# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in DocFlux respectful and harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and discuss technical disagreements with evidence.
- Focus on improving the project and helping contributors succeed.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, abuse, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory or hateful language
- Trolling or intentionally disruptive behavior

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately via the security/contact channel described in `SECURITY.md`.

All reports will be reviewed and handled promptly and fairly.
